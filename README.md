# Snuffers

**Student:** Tusti Tanaya Saharia

## Project Overview

Snuffers is a fictional dog-fashion platform for dog owners looking for **stylish, comfortable, inclusive, and reasonably priced clothing** for their dogs. It offers outfits for everyday outings and special occasions, along with customised outfit options.

The idea is personal to me — **Snuffers is named after my dog, Snuffy.** I chose this concept because I had personally looked for chic and fashionable outfits for my dog that were also comfortable and budget-friendly.

## Problem It Solves

From my personal experience, many fashionable dog outfits are designed mainly for petite or smaller dogs. I wanted to make the same chic look more accessible to **dogs of different breeds, shapes, and sizes**, while keeping the products comfortable and reasonably priced.

## Target User

Dog owners who want to dress their dogs in stylish clothing but want a convenient destination where the clothing is fashionable, comfortable, appropriately sized, inclusive of different body shapes, and reasonably priced.

## Core Job

> A dog owner opens Snuffers to find a stylish outfit that will actually fit their dog.

## Key Features

- Product discovery
- Category filtering
- Chest-girth finder
- Inclusive sizing
- Product details
- Matching-size highlighting
- Colour and size selection
- Favourite
- Add to Bag
- Custom outfit promotion

## Product Scope

The final catalogue contains **18 fictional products**:

- **12** dog clothing products
- **3** dog shoe products
- **3** dog treat products

## Design Direction

The design focuses on making fashionable dog clothing feel inclusive across different breeds, shapes, and sizes. Similar styles are presented across different dog types rather than limiting fashionable designs to smaller dogs.

The visual direction is **aesthetically pleasing, chic, stylish, modern, and fashion-focused**, while remaining approachable and affordable.

## Technology / Development

- **Google Stitch** — interface and visual design
- **Google AI Studio** — implementation and refinement


## Scope & Limitations

This is a frontend prototype and does not include:

- Login or accounts
- Checkout or payment
- Order processing
- Database or backend
- Live data
- External APIs or services
- Web scraping
- Gemini/API functionality
